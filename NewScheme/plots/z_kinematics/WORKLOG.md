# Run-2 Z kinematic plots

Combined 2016preAPV, 2016postAPV, 2017 and 2018, electron and muon channels, and all three exclusive tags, separately for resolved and merged selections. Used full nominal MC weights, including DY ParticleNet SFs. Luminosity label is 138 fb^-1; original era normalization is preserved.

Produced 16 individual distributions (four variables, two topologies, SR and full CR), four overview pages, and a 16-page PDF. Each figure also has a PNG. Source script, histogram cache, per-input yield audit and source metadata are retained.

SR Data files were not opened. CR includes 105–135 GeV and VR; VR trees are not added separately. CR panels include observed data and Data/MC ratios. The background band is MC statistical uncertainty from summed squared event weights; it is not a systematic-uncertainty envelope.

Validation passed for 336 inputs, all four variables' complete selected yields, absence of SR Data histograms, retained CR bridge/VR events, and 20 PNG/21 PDF outputs. Plot-only rerendering uses the histogram cache.
